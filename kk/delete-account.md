# Account Deletion — Arcanum Lux

**App:** Arcanum Lux
**Developer:** Individual Entrepreneur R. I. Nuriyev
**Last updated:** 31.08.2026

This page explains how to delete your Arcanum Lux account and what data is
deleted when you do.

---

## Option 1. Delete directly in the app (instant)

1. Open the **Arcanum Lux** app
2. Go to the **"Settings"** tab (far right in the bottom menu)
3. Scroll down and tap **"Delete account"**
4. Confirm the deletion. If you signed up with email and password, you will be
   asked to enter your password
5. Done: your account and all data are deleted immediately and irreversibly

---

## Option 2. Request deletion by email

If you no longer have access to the app (for example, you removed it from your
phone), write to us:

**nurro.dev@gmail.com**

Include the **email address your account is registered to**. We will process
the request within **30 days** and confirm the deletion by reply.

---

## What data is deleted

Deleted **immediately and irreversibly**, with no retention period:

| Data |
|---|
| Account: email, name, date of birth |
| Onboarding survey answers (interests) |
| Settings: interface language, notification time |
| All reading history and cards of the day |
| All personal journal notes |
| Full-access purchase records |
| Records of acceptance of the legal documents |
| Device tokens for push notifications |

---

## What data is retained, and why

| Data | Held by | Retention |
|---|---|---|
| Technical app error reports (device type, OS version, error description — **no email, no contents of your notes**) | Sentry | up to 90 days, then deleted automatically |
| Payment history | Google Play / Apple | per the respective store's policy — we have no access to this data and cannot delete it |
| Anonymised advertising data | Google AdMob | per [Google's policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) |

We do not keep copies of deleted accounts and cannot restore them — deletion is
irreversible.

---

## Important note about purchases

Deleting your account does **not refund** the money paid for full access. For
refund matters, contact Google Play or the App Store — they process payments,
not us.

If you delete your account and register again, previously purchased full access
can be restored through the app store ("Restore purchases").
